# Personas

The stress personas the Compliance Adversary uses to walk compliance work and
find blind spots. Each is a distinct way the work can fail.

## The Regulator

must answer for the work under audit. Is it compliant and documented?

## The Auditor

must verify the work. Is there evidence and an audit trail?

## The Operator

must run the compliant system. Can they prove compliance on demand?

## The Skeptic

reads the compliance claim as a hostile reviewer. Which claim dies on first contact?
