# Personas

The stress personas the Ops & Reliability Adversary uses to walk ops work and
find blind spots. Each is a distinct way the work can fail.

## The First Deployer

deploys the change first. Can they do it safely and undo it?

## The Midnight Pager

gets woken at 3am when it fails. Can they diagnose and fix it?

## The Operator

must run the system. Can they observe it and recover it?

## The Skeptic

reads the ops claim as a hostile reviewer. Which claim dies on first contact?
