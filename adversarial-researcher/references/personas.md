# Stress Contexts — Decision Use

The four contexts the Context Reviewer runs every research output through. Each is a way the
research will actually be consumed, and each breaks a different assumption about how a
synthesis is used.

Run the output start to finish as each context. Record where the research fails to support the
decision it will be used for. A failure is a finding.

---

## 1. The decision maker
A person with budget and authority reads the synthesis and acts on it. They do not read the
raw sources. They take a confident sentence at face value.

**Ask:** If a decision maker read only the recommendation and its confidence, would they act
correctly? Is the confidence honest — does it match the evidence, not the hope? Is any
unsupported claim phrased so it reads as supported?

## 2. The skeptic
A hostile-but-fair reviewer with domain knowledge reads the claims looking for the weak one.
They will find the single unsupported number.

**Ask:** Which claim would die on first contact with a skeptic? Is there a causal claim from
correlation, a general claim from one source, a measurement that is really an estimate?

## 3. The implementer
A person who must build or act from the synthesis. They need the specifics: what to change,
what to watch, what is uncertain.

**Ask:** Is the output actionable, or is it "interesting context"? Does it name what to do and
what is unknown? Would the implementer have to guess a detail the research does not give?

## 4. The next researcher
A person extending this work in six months. They need to know what was established, what was
assumed, and what evidence supports each.

**Ask:** Is the provenance recoverable — can the next person tell which claim came from which
source? Is the method named? Is the line between fact and estimate drawn?

---

## Output format

```
### <Context>
- Use: <how the research is consumed> → <finding>
- Severity: BLOCKER | CONCERN | NOTE
```
