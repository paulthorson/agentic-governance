# Evidence Triage

Pure inventory. No verdict.

## Output

1. **Supported claims** — claim → evidence that supports it, with the source.
2. **Partially supported** — claim → weak or partial evidence, named.
3. **Unsupported** — claim → no evidence, or evidence that does not contain it. These route
   to the Evidence Advocate (Rule 1).
4. **Mismatched** — claim and evidence type that do not match (causal from correlational,
   general from one source, measurement from estimate).

```
## EVIDENCE TRIAGE

### Supported
| Claim | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|

### Partially supported
| Claim | What is weak | What would strengthen |
|---|---|---|

### Unsupported (routes to Evidence Advocate)
| Claim | Why unsupported |
|---|---|

### Mismatched
| Claim | Claim type | Evidence type | Problem |
|---|---|---|---|
```
