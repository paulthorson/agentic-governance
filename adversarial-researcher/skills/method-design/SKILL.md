# Method Design

Pure artifact. No verdict, and nothing is run.

## Output

1. **Question** — the reframed question.
2. **Approach** — what would establish the answer, and what it trades away.
3. **Instrument** — the survey, interview guide, test, or search plan.
4. **Sample / scope** — who or what is studied, and the limits.
5. **What would remain unknown** — what the method cannot establish, named.
