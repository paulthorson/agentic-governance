# Learning 2026-09-13 — Blind-review isolation against transcript inheritance

## What happened

Reviewer subagents inherit the coordinator's full transcript — including worker
rationale, briefs, and prior verdicts. A reviewer who reads the builder's
justification before judging the work product is not blind.

## Learning

Blind review under inherited transcripts requires an explicit instruction in the
reviewer brief: judge the work product alone, ignore any inherited worker
rationale. The brief contains the work product and the generated review prompt —
nothing else.

## What changed

Framework convention: every reviewer brief carries the isolation instruction.
Fresh reviewers on every re-review (a reviewer never re-reviews work they kicked
back).
