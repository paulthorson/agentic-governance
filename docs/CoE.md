# Center of Excellence

Agentic Governance is a standing standard plus feedback from shipped work.

The standard lives in this repository: constitution, role guides, adversary checks, and the calibration ledger. Teams ship under that published standard. Retrospective notes from real work feed the next revision.

This page describes the product operating model. It does not add a separate bot or rewrite the constitution.

## Loop

1. A team ships under the published standard.
2. The team files a short retrospective (what worked, what did not, what to improve).
3. Named follow-ups land as ordinary product work.
4. Teams use the next published revision.
