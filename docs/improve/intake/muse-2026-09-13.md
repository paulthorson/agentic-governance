## Proposal (SUPERSEDED / historical / not-live): UX flows, JTBD, research validation

> **SUPERSEDED.** Historical intake only. Superseded by LIVE Check 7 SoT
> (Cos ACCEPT #14 @ `36deb0e`). Not an amendment path. Not unpaid
> constitution work. `LIVE_SOT_MERGED_SHA` governs; intake does not.

**Problem (historical).** At intake time, the UX constitution
(`constitution/domains/ux.md`) enforced four mechanical rules — customer
vetoes, genuine options, engineering-ease disclosure, business-goal linkage —
all checked against the decision record. It did not yet require evidence that
the worker understood the user before designing: no user flows for key
journeys, no Jobs-To-Be-Done grounding, no mapping of decisions to existing
research. In a live design-system monorepo build, the human operator had to
stop the build and explicitly demand user flows, a JTBD framework, and
validation against the project's research.

**What was proposed (now LIVE elsewhere).** Add a standing UX-domain check:

1. **User flows are mandatory artifacts** (Mermaid; entry/success/error-empty/exits).
2. **JTBD job stories ground every major decision.**
3. **Research validation is explicit** (cite Research or `NO_RESEARCH`→human).

Those requirements shipped as harness + Critic Check 7 in #14 @ `36deb0e`.
This intake section remains only as the historical record of the operator
demand that preceded that merge.
