# A18.3 Clean Test — Validation Record

**Date:** 2026-09-06
**Rule under test:** A18.3 — the CEO bot restates a solution-framed objective as a problem; passing it through verbatim is not scoping.
**Method:** Isolated CEO subagent. Received ONLY the solution-framed objective plus the CEO harness and constitution. **No instruction to restate** — the harness's A18.3 rule must drive the restatement on its own.

## Objective given (solution-framed)

> "Add a `--json` flag to the stuck-review watchdog script so downstream tooling can parse its output."

## Result: PASS

The CEO subagent, unprompted, did the following:

1. **Recognized the objective was solution-framed:** "The objective presumes the answer — a `--json` flag — before the underlying problem is understood."
2. **Restated it as a problem:** "downstream tooling cannot reliably parse the stuck-review watchdog's output today, and we do not yet know what those consumers actually need."
3. **Scoped a genuine research question** around the problem space (current output contract, downstream consumers, failure/harm surface, genuine alternatives, schema/stability, completeness) rather than passing the objective through verbatim.

## Why this is clean (vs. the A11c contamination)

In the A11c run, the CEO was sent the solution-framed objective **plus an explicit A18.3 instruction** to restate it, so the restatement was driven by the prompt, not the harness. In this run, the CEO received **no such instruction** — the restatement was driven solely by the harness's A18.3 rule. This is the first uncontaminated evidence that the rule works.

## Caveat

Single run, single model (deepseek-v4-flash:cloud). A stronger claim would repeat across models/contexts, but this establishes that the harness rule, not the operator's prompt, can drive the restatement.
