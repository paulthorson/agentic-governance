# Personas

The stress personas the Data & Privacy Adversary uses to walk privacy work and
find blind spots. Each is a distinct way the work can fail.

## The Data Subject

the person whose data is collected. Can they see, correct, and delete it?

## The Regulator

must answer for the data handling under audit. Is it lawful, bounded, and documented?

## The Operator

must run and maintain the data pipeline. Can they honor a deletion request?

## The Skeptic

reads the data handling as a hostile reviewer. Which privacy claim dies on first contact?
