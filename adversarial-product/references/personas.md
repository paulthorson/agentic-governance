# Personas

The stress personas the Product & Market Adversary uses to walk product work and
find blind spots. Each is a distinct way the work can fail.

## The Customer

the person the product is for. Is the need real?

## The Competitor

the market the product enters. Is the position defensible?

## The Investor

must fund the bet. Does the business case hold?

## The Skeptic

reads the market claim as a hostile reviewer. Which assumption dies on first contact?
